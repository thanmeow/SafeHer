Before you start

This tool helps you look for hidden cameras but cannot guarantee a room is safe. Results may not be accurate.

If something feels wrong, trust your instinct: leave and ask for a different room or call 112.

We'll show the hiding spots most common for this kind of room.

Hotel room 0/12 checked

    Two-way mirror: fingernail test

    1. Place the tip of your fingernail straight against the mirror.
    2. Gap between your nail and its reflection → most likely a normal mirror (the reflective layer is behind the glass).
    3. Nail seems to touch its reflection, no gap → it could be a two-way mirror. Be cautious.

    Some ordinary mirrors also show no gap, so double-check: turn off the lights, cup your hands around your eyes and press them against the mirror — if you can see into a space behind it, it's two-way. A mirror that sounds hollow when tapped, or is set into the wall instead of hanging on it, is also a warning sign.

    Lens reflection scan

    1. Turn off the room lights and close the curtains.
    2. Shine a torch or a second phone's flashlight from right beside your eyes.
    3. Point this phone at suspect objects and move slowly. Camera lenses reflect light back as a small, sharp, bright dot.
    4. Red circles mark small, very bright spots. Check those areas closely — many will be harmless (screws, LEDs, glossy plastic).

    Infrared (night-vision) check

    1. Make the room as dark as possible.
    2. This uses the front camera — on many iPhones the rear camera filters out infrared.
    3. Slowly pan around the room. Look for purple or white glowing dots that you can't see with your eyes.

    Tip: test first by pointing a TV remote at the front camera and pressing a button. If you see the remote's light flash, your camera can see infrared.

      One-press SOS

      The SOS Alert shortcut runs with a single press, even from the Lock Screen. When triggered it can share your current location with your trusted contacts by message and start a call to 112.

      Install SOS Shortcut

      The shortcut link hasn't been set up yet (SOS_SHORTCUT_URL in app.js).

      After installing

      1. Open Shortcuts, tap ⋯ on SOS Alert, tap Recipients and add your emergency contacts, then make sure Show When Run is off.
      2. Run it once and choose Always Allow for location and messages.
      3. Attach it to one of the triggers below.

      Set up a trigger

      Action Button iPhone 15 Pro and later
      1. Open Settings → Action Button.
      2. Swipe through the options until you reach Shortcut.
      3. Tap Choose a Shortcut and pick SOS Alert.
      4. Press and hold the Action Button to test it.
      Back Tap iPhone 8 and later
      1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap.
      2. Choose Triple Tap (less likely to trigger by accident than double tap).
      3. Scroll down to Shortcuts and pick SOS Alert.
      4. Tap the back of your phone three times firmly to test.
      Home Screen widget
      1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until icons jiggle.
      2. Tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Shortcuts.
      3. Choose the small single-shortcut widget and tap Add Widget.
      4. Touch and hold the widget → Edit Widget → choose SOS Alert.
      5. Place it where your thumb reaches easily.
      Lock Screen control iOS 18 and later
      1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen.
      2. Tap the − on one of the bottom controls (torch or camera) to remove it, then tap +.
      3. Search for Shortcut, add it, then tap it to choose SOS Alert.
      4. Tap Done. You can also add the same control to Control Center (swipe down → + → Add a Control).
      Siri
      1. Siri runs a shortcut by its name. Say “Hey Siri, SOS Alert”.
      2. To make it work when locked: Settings → Siri → turn on Allow Siri When Locked.
      3. Tip: rename the shortcut to something easy to say quickly, like “Help me now”.

      Backup: iPhone Emergency SOS

      Holding the side button and a volume button calls emergency services and alerts your Emergency Contacts. Set it up in Settings → Emergency SOS and add contacts in the Health app → Medical ID.

      Test checklist

      Tell your contacts before testing. If your shortcut calls 112, edit it to call a friend while you test.

        Possible hidden camera

        1. Don't touch it. Moving it can destroy evidence and alert whoever placed it.
        2. Take photos from a distance showing the device and where it is in the room.
        3. Leave the room calmly. Don't confront anyone on your own.
        4. Report it to management or the owner, and to the police. In an emergency, call 112.
