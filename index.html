Before you start

This tool helps you look for hidden cameras but cannot guarantee a room is safe. Results may not be accurate.

If something feels wrong, leave and ask for another room, or call 112.

Choose the kind of room. Each type has its own list of places to check.

    Hotel room 0/12 checked

      Two-way mirror: fingernail test

      1. Place the tip of your fingernail straight against the mirror.
      2. Gap between your nail and its reflection: most likely a normal mirror. The reflective layer is behind the glass.
      3. No gap: it could be a two-way mirror. Be cautious.

      Some normal mirrors also show no gap. To double-check, turn off the lights, cup your hands around your eyes and press them against the mirror. If you can see a space behind it, it is two-way. A mirror that sounds hollow, or is set into the wall instead of hanging on it, is also a warning sign.

      Lens reflection scan

      1. Turn off the lights and close the curtains.
      2. Hold a torch or a second phone's flashlight right beside your eyes.
      3. Point this phone at objects and move slowly. A camera lens reflects light back as a small, sharp, bright dot.
      4. Red circles mark small, very bright spots. Check those areas closely. Many will be harmless, such as screws, LEDs or glossy plastic.

      Camera is off

      Infrared (night-vision) check

      1. Make the room as dark as possible.
      2. This uses the front camera. On many iPhones the rear camera filters out infrared.
      3. Pan slowly around the room. Look for purple or white glowing dots you can't see with your eyes.

      Test first: point a TV remote at the front camera and press a button. If you see the remote's light flash, your camera can see infrared.

          A. If you are in danger right now
          Call 112
          1. Call 112.
          2. Get to a safe place: a shop, a police station, a hospital, or anywhere with people around.
          3. Tell someone you trust where you are.
          4. When you are safe, record what happened in the Vault while it is fresh.
          B. File a police complaintChecklist

          Your rights

          • You can file an FIR at any police station. If it happened elsewhere, a Zero FIR is registered and transferred (BNSS Section 173).
          • For a cognizable offence, the police must register the FIR.
          • You get a free copy of the FIR.
          • For sexual offences, your statement should be recorded by a woman officer.
          • If the police refuse, write to the Superintendent of Police, or apply to the Magistrate.

          Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023. Old IPC sections in brackets.

          F. Strengthen your case
          • Record incidents as soon as possible, with exact dates and times.
          • Keep originals. Don’t edit, crop or forward screenshots in a way that loses detail.
          • Note witnesses and their contact details.
          • Ask for CCTV footage quickly. It is often overwritten within weeks.
          • Electronic evidence used in court may need a certificate under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam. Keep the original device or files.
          • Get a medical examination soon after any physical assault.
          • Keep copies of every complaint, acknowledgement and FIR number.

          Security

          How it works

          Unlocking

          The app always opens as a calculator. Type your 4-digit PIN, then press =.

          Quick exit

          Tap Quick exit in the bottom bar to switch to the calculator straight away. The screen you were on is closed.

          Forgot PIN

          A PIN cannot be recovered. On the calculator, hold AC for 5 seconds. This wipes all data so you can set a new PIN.

          Storage

          Everything stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded or backed up. Deleting the app or clearing Safari website data deletes it, so export a report from the Vault to keep a copy.

          Delete everything?

          This permanently deletes your PIN, all incidents and photos, checklists and settings on this phone. It cannot be undone.

          One-press SOS

          The SOS Alert shortcut runs with one press, even from the Lock Screen. It can send your location to your trusted contacts and start a call to 112.

          Install SOS shortcut

          The shortcut link is not set up yet (SOS_SHORTCUT_URL in app.js).

          After installing

          1. Open Shortcuts, tap ⋯ on SOS Alert, tap Recipients and add your emergency contacts, then make sure Show When Run is off.
          2. Run it once and choose Always Allow for location and messages.
          3. Attach it to one of the triggers below.

          Set up a trigger

          Action ButtoniPhone 15 Pro and later
          1. Open Settings → Action Button.
          2. Swipe through the options to Shortcut.
          3. Tap Choose a Shortcut and pick SOS Alert.
          4. Press and hold the Action Button to test it.
          Back TapiPhone 8 and later
          1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap.
          2. Choose Triple Tap. It is less likely to trigger by accident than Double Tap.
          3. Scroll down to Shortcuts and pick SOS Alert.
          4. Tap the back of your phone firmly three times to test.
          Home Screen widget
          1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
          2. Tap Edit → Add Widget and search for Shortcuts.
          3. Choose the small single-shortcut widget and tap Add Widget.
          4. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and choose SOS Alert.
          5. Place it where your thumb reaches easily.
          Lock Screen controliOS 18 and later
          1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize → Lock Screen.
          2. Tap − on one of the bottom controls (torch or camera) to remove it, then tap +.
          3. Search for Shortcut, add it, then tap it and choose SOS Alert.
          4. Tap Done. You can add the same control to Control Center: swipe down, tap +, then Add a Control.
          Siri
          1. Siri runs a shortcut by its name. Say “Hey Siri, SOS Alert”.
          2. To use it when locked, go to Settings → Siri and turn on Allow Siri When Locked.
          3. You can rename the shortcut to something quick to say, such as “Help me now”.

          Backup: iPhone Emergency SOS

          Holding the side button and a volume button calls emergency services and alerts your emergency contacts. Set it up in Settings → Emergency SOS, and add contacts in the Health app under Medical ID.

          Test checklist

          Tell your contacts before you test. If your shortcut calls 112, change it to call a friend while testing.

            Possible hidden camera

            1. Don't touch it. Moving it can destroy evidence and alert whoever placed it.
            2. Take photos from a distance that show the device and where it is in the room.
            3. Leave the room calmly. Don't confront anyone on your own.
            4. Report it to management or the owner, and to the police. In an emergency, call 112.
